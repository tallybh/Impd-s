# IMPD Quality — Part S: Drug Substance

## S.2 Manufacture

This section identifies the organizations and controlled records associated with manufacture of the PSIGA2 Drug Substance, describes the manufacturing scope at a non-operational level, and documents the supporting material-control, process-control, evaluation, and batch-comparability evidence. Manufacturing conditions and technical instructions are contained in the manufacturer’s approved records, not generated or inferred in this dossier.

The starting material is cultivated fruiting bodies of *Psilocybe cubensis* designated PSIGA2. The Drug Substance is the dried, multicomponent botanical extract blend containing the added anti-caking agent, as defined in S.1.1. The native extract, final Drug Substance blend, and finished oral capsules are separate material stages. The capsules are described in Part P.
