# IMPD Quality — Part S: Drug Substance

## S.6 Container Closure System

**Product:** PsygaBio standardized *Psilocybe cubensis* extract (PSIGA2). **Material covered:** final dried Drug Substance blend, including the added anti-caking agent, prior to manufacture of the finished capsules. **Responsible organization:** PsygaBio (S.2.1).

This section describes the container–closure system used to hold the final Drug Substance and summarizes the documented basis for its selection, control, and suitability during storage. The specifications and evidence for this system are presented directly in Sections S.6.1 and S.6.2. Storage conditions and stability results will be linked to S.7 and the complete supporting stability records in Appendix F. The container–closure system for finished capsules is addressed separately in Part P.7.
