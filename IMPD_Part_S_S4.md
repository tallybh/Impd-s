# IMPD Quality — Part S: Drug Substance

## S.4 Control of Drug Substance

The quality documentation for the PSIGA2 Drug Substance will link the final extract blend defined in S.1 to the controlled specification, applicable analytical records, and batch-specific findings. The Drug Substance comprises the dried, multicomponent *Psilocybe cubensis* extract with the added anti-caking agent. The material scope and reporting conventions established in S.1 and S.3 apply throughout this section.

PsygaBio is the responsible organization for Drug Substance manufacturing and in-house quality testing at 9 Tarshish Street, Caesarea Industrial Park, Caesarea, Israel (S.2.1). Controlled records of quality responsibilities are referenced in Appendix H. The evidence described below will be presented through the specification and batch certificates in Appendix D and the underlying method and analytical documentation in Appendix E.

### S.4.1 Specification

The Drug Substance specification will document the identity, constituent composition, impurity controls, and relevant physical and microbiological attributes of the final blend. The sponsor-defined nominal psilocybin-equivalent content is **5% w/w**, reported on the as-is basis for the final blend, including the anti-caking agent. This nominal value is within the **4–6% w/w** defined potency range described in S.1.5. The approved criteria and associated certificates of analysis will be presented in Appendix D; batch-specific evidence will be summarized in S.4.4. The characterization supporting these controls is presented in S.3, and their scientific justification is addressed in S.4.5.
