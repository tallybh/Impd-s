# IMPD Quality — Part S: Drug Substance

## S.3 Characterization

This section characterizes the identity, composition, and quality-related constituents of the PSIGA2 Drug Substance. The material under assessment is the final dried, multicomponent *Psilocybe cubensis* extract blend containing the added anti-caking agent, as defined in S.1. Published findings about whole mushrooms, other strains, or other fungal preparations provide scientific context; they are not represented as measured characteristics of the PSIGA2 final blend. Product-specific conclusions are linked to controlled analytical and batch records.

PsygaBio performs the production and quality-testing activities at its site at 9 Tarshish Street, Caesarea Industrial Park, Caesarea, Israel (S.2.1). This statement of organizational responsibility does not, by itself, establish the results of particular analyses. Specifications, validated procedure records, batch analyses, and stability findings are addressed in S.4 and S.7.

### S.3.1 Identity, Composition and Other Characteristics

#### S.3.1.1 Botanical and Chemical Identity

The fungal source species is *Psilocybe cubensis* (Earle) Singer; the working production-strain designation is PSIGA2. The source material is cultivated fruiting bodies. The botanical identity of the starting material and the relationship of the production culture to the designated PSIGA2 reference culture are separate evidentiary questions. Species identification does not, by itself, verify the identity of a particular production culture.

The final Drug Substance is distinguished from the native extract and the starting fruiting bodies. Identification of the final material therefore rests on a combination of source traceability and controlled product-specific characterization. Botanical and culture-identification evidence is documented in S.1.2 and Appendix B, while the applicable analytical records are cross-referenced in Appendix E.

#### S.3.1.2 Psilocybin and Psilocin Characterization

Psilocybin and psilocin are the principal designated quantitative markers of the Drug Substance. Their individual concentrations are reported separately; the project-defined psilocybin-equivalent convention is described in S.1.5. These related constituents are chemically distinct, and reporting them separately allows composition and changes in constituent proportions to be assessed without relying solely on a composite value.

Research on *P. cubensis* fruiting bodies confirms the occurrence of psilocybin and psilocin, while also documenting variation among strains and individual fruiting bodies [2, 5]. Those observations support the relevance of product-specific characterization but do not establish concentrations, reproducibility, or stability in PSIGA2.

#### S.3.1.3 Additional Alkaloids and Chromatographic Fingerprint

Baeocystin, norbaeocystin, and aeruginascin are designated additional alkaloids for the PSIGA2 characterization plan. Published chemical investigations of *Psilocybe* materials have documented related tryptamines and variation in their occurrence among fungal samples [4, 5]. Presence in a published *P. cubensis* sample does not demonstrate presence or a comparable relative abundance in the PSIGA2 final blend. An absence, non-detection, or result below a quantitation limit must be reported according to the actual analytical finding rather than recorded as zero.

#### S.3.1.5 Standardization Basis and Expression of Assay Results

The Drug Substance is reported as a standardized multicomponent extract blend, not as a purified single-molecule substance. As approved for this dossier, **assay results are expressed on an as-is basis as % w/w of the final Drug Substance blend, including the anti-caking agent**. Psilocybin and psilocin are reported individually; the composite psilocybin-equivalent result follows the project-defined convention in S.1.5. This analytical reporting convention does not establish clinical equivalence of individual constituents or other preparations.

The 4–6% w/w range appearing in earlier project documentation remains a working Drug Substance range until linked to a controlled, approved specification and supporting batch results. The confirmed capsule declaration is 5 mg psilocybin equivalents per capsule and belongs to the Drug Product documentation in Part P; it is not a substitute for the Drug Substance assay records.

### S.3.2 Impurities and Contaminants

Impurities and contaminants are assessed separately from constituents intentionally included in the characterized botanical matrix. Their identity, occurrence and toxicological relevance depend on the actual source materials, processing history, analytical evidence and exposure context. General fungal literature and regulatory guidance identify relevant categories for evaluation but cannot establish compliance or absence in a PSIGA2 batch. Applicable test results and specifications belong in S.4, with supporting reports in Appendices D and E.
