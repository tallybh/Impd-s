# IMPD Quality — Part S: Drug Substance

## S.7 Stability

**Product:** PsygaBio Standardized *Psilocybe cubensis* Extract (PSIGA2).  
**Material assessed:** Final dried Drug Substance blend, including the added anti-caking agent.  
**Responsible organization:** PsygaBio.  
**Scope:** Stability of the Drug Substance before its incorporation into the finished capsules. Finished-product stability is addressed separately in Part P.8.

The stability evaluation will establish the relationship between storage time, the documented container–closure system, and the quality attributes of the final PSIGA2 Drug Substance blend. The program has an explicit **three-month assessment target**. Batch-specific study conditions, observations, and resulting conclusions will be presented in Sections S.7.1–S.7.3, supported by the controlled stability records in **Appendix F — Stability Protocols and Results**. The container–closure system is described in S.6, and the analytical specifications and batch-release controls are addressed in S.4.

ICH Q1A(R2) and ICH Q1E provide internationally recognized scientific frameworks for documenting stability studies and evaluating stability data [11, 12]. The applicable Israeli Ministry of Health requirements govern the submission. The study design and documentary conclusions will reflect the actual development stage and the approved PSIGA2 study records.

**Potency convention.** The sponsor-defined nominal content is **5% w/w psilocybin equivalents**, expressed on an **as-is basis for the final Drug Substance blend**. The product-defined range described in S.1.5 is **4–6% w/w**; the controlled specification and applicability of that range to stability assessment will be documented in S.4.1 and Appendix D. Individual measured psilocybin and psilocin contents and their calculated psilocybin-equivalent total will be shown for each tested batch and timepoint in S.7.3. The assay calculation is: **psilocybin equivalents = psilocybin + (1.39 × psilocin)**, using consistent units and mass basis (S.1.5).
