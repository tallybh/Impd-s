# IMPD Quality — Part S: Drug Substance

## S.1 General Information

### S.1.1 Nomenclature and Drug Substance Definition

The working drug substance is PsygaBio Standardized Psilocybe cubensis Extract, a dried, partially purified, standardized multicomponent fungal preparation derived from the fruiting bodies of Psilocybe cubensis. PSIGA2 is the project strain designation. The cultivated fruiting bodies are the fungal starting material, whereas the final Drug Substance is the dried extract blend containing an added anti-caking agent.

The native extract, final Drug Substance blend, and finished oral capsules are distinct material stages for quality documentation. The Drug Substance is not isolated or synthetic psilocybin. Its potency is expressed as psilocybin equivalents; the declared capsule content is 5 mg psilocybin equivalents per capsule, confirmed for the project under validated analytical conditions. Supporting product-specific documentation remains cross-referenced below.

### S.1.5 Potency Expression and Psilocybin Equivalents

The potency reporting convention is based on the separately measured contents of psilocybin and psilocin, with a project-defined conversion factor of 1.39 applied to psilocin for reporting as psilocybin equivalents. This factor describes a mass-based analytical convention; it does not, by itself, demonstrate pharmacological or clinical equivalence between constituents or preparations.

The approved assay reporting basis for this dossier is: As-is basis, expressed as % w/w of the final Drug Substance blend, including the anti-caking agent. Individual analyte contents and the corresponding psilocybin-equivalent result must use the same mass basis.

The previously supplied 4–6% w/w range is retained as a working Drug Substance definition, pending the approved specification and supporting batch results. The separately confirmed Drug Product capsule content is 5 mg psilocybin equivalents per capsule. Batch-level supporting reports and controlled specifications are referenced below; this section does not establish a batch-filling procedure.
