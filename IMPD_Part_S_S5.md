# IMPD Quality — Part S: Drug Substance

## S.5 Reference Standards or Materials

**Product:** PsygaBio standardized *Psilocybe cubensis* extract (PSIGA2). **Material:** final dried Drug Substance blend, including the added anti-caking agent (S.1.1). **Responsible organization:** PsygaBio (S.2.1).

This section consolidates the reference-material information used to support the identity and constituent measurements of the PSIGA2 Drug Substance. Material designations, source and lot traceability, certified or assigned values, qualification status, and controlled document identifiers are presented directly in Sections S.5.1–S.5.4. Appendix G provides the dossier-wide index of cited controlled records.

Reference-material documentation will be linked to the specific analytical procedure and to the PSIGA2 batch for which the reference was used. Each TBD entry below identifies the evidence required to complete the regulatory record from existing approved source documentation.

### S.5.1 Psilocybin and Psilocin Reference Standards

Psilocybin and psilocin are the primary quantitative markers in the PSIGA2 Drug Substance assay. Their individually measured contents support the psilocybin-equivalent potency expression defined in S.1.5: **psilocybin equivalents = psilocybin + (1.39 × psilocin)**, with both analytes expressed on the same as-is mass basis. The applicable analytical procedures will be identified in S.4.2 and their batch-specific results in S.4.4.
